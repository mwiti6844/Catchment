Privacy request

Request data deletion

You may ask Catchment to delete information associated with your WhatsApp interactions.

01

Send your request

Email david.mwiti.muthiru@gmail.com with the subject Catchment Data Deletion Request.

02

Identify the relevant data

Include the WhatsApp number associated with the request, the approximate dates of interaction, and a short description of what you want deleted. Do not send passwords, verification codes, access tokens, or unrelated sensitive documents.

03

Complete verification

Catchment may ask you to verify control of the relevant WhatsApp number before disclosing or deleting information.

04

Deletion and confirmation

Verified requests will be handled without undue delay, ordinarily within 30 days. Catchment will confirm completion or explain any information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud prevention, or backup-cycle reasons.

What deletion covers

Where applicable, deletion includes stored messages, extracted text, embeddings, tag assignments, and source metadata linked to the verified WhatsApp identity. De-identified operational records that cannot reasonably be linked back to you may be retained.
