Privacy policy

This policy describes how Catchment processes information received through its WhatsApp Business Platform integration and related services.

1. Who operates Catchment

Catchment is a privately operated personal content-intelligence project managed by David Mwiti. Privacy questions may be sent to david.mwiti.muthiru@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

When you communicate with the designated Catchment WhatsApp number, Catchment may process:

  • message text and media captions;
  • your WhatsApp number and profile name;
  • message identifiers, type, and timestamps;
  • media identifiers and technical metadata supplied by WhatsApp;
  • derived information such as text extractions, classifications, tags, and semantic representations; and
  • limited operational information needed to secure and troubleshoot the service, such as event counts, internal item identifiers, and content length or cryptographic digests.

Catchment does not intentionally place message bodies, transcripts, credentials, or access tokens in normal informational logs.

3. How information is used

Information is processed to:

  • receive and preserve content deliberately sent to Catchment;
  • extract, organize, classify, search, and retrieve that content;
  • develop recommendations and research features at the operator’s direction;
  • deduplicate messages and operate asynchronous processing jobs;
  • verify webhook authenticity, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing and service providers

Catchment does not sell personal information and does not use it for third-party advertising. Information may be handled by service providers strictly to operate the project, including Meta and WhatsApp for message delivery, hosting or tunneling providers for network transport, and infrastructure used for databases, job processing, backups, and model observability.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, or investigate abuse.

5. Retention

Ingested content and its derived records are retained while needed for the personal knowledge system or until deletion is requested. Temporary processing records may remain for operational recovery, security, or backup purposes and are removed or overwritten according to the applicable system’s retention cycle.

6. Security

Catchment uses controls including signed-webhook verification, restricted secrets, database constraints, content-aware log redaction, and limited-access infrastructure. No storage or transmission system is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask whether Catchment holds information associated with your WhatsApp number, request correction or deletion, or object to further processing. Catchment may request reasonable verification before acting to prevent unauthorized access or deletion.

Follow the data-deletion instructions or email the privacy contact above.

8. Other people’s information

Do not send Catchment confidential information about another person unless you have permission or another lawful basis to do so. The sender is responsible for deciding what content to submit.

9. Children

Catchment is not directed to children and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

10. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where a message originated, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards.

11. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as Catchment’s capabilities or obligations change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published.
